Cards in trophy decks

Every card that made the main deck of a Premier Draft trophy deck, ranked by how many of those decks played it. Decklists and picks are from 17Lands trophy decks; rarity is from Scryfall. Open a card to see the decks that played it.

Main deck only. Copies in the sideboard don't count. Played is the share of drafted copies that made the main deck. Rare cards show up less often simply because there are fewer of them, so look at take rate to see how highly drafters rated a card when they had the chance. The sample is small (– decks), so treat it as directional.
