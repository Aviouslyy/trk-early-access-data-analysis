How to read this

  • Small sample. This covers the first day or so of early access. One or two decks can change a colour pair's ranking, so treat it as directional.
  • Trophies are not win rates. A colour pair can top the trophy count just because more people drafted it. Colour-pair and card win rates aren't published for this set yet. For those, use the colour ratings and card data pages on 17Lands once they fill in.
  • Who's in the data. Only 17Lands users running its tracker appear here. They tend to be more experienced than the average Arena player.
  • Colour notation. Upper-case letters are main colours and small pips are splashes, as 17Lands writes them. For example, "WBg" is white-black splashing green.
  • Leaderboard. It lists a limited set of players ranked by wins and trophies, so its trophy total can be lower than the trophy-deck count. The highest win rates come from players with very few games.
